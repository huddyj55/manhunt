HOW TO PLAY

Chop enemies only when they cross onto YOUR side. Chopped players are out until a teammate brings an enemy flag home. Wipe out the other team to win.

BATTLE 5:00 · QUICK 2:00 · 3 TEAMS three teams, three bases: grab any enemy flag, last team standing wins.

KEYBOARD: WASD / ARROWS move · SHIFT sprint · SPACE jump · Q/E dive · J or CLICK chop · M map · P or ESC pause / exit
XBOX: stick move · LB/LT sprint · A jump · B dive · X/RT chop · Y map · START pause / exit
TOUCH: drag left side to move · hold RUN · JUMP · DIVE · CHOP · tap minimap for map · II pause / exit

Dive to escape — you can't dive into water. Sprinting, jumping and diving use ENERGY. You can't be chopped mid-jump or mid-dive. Enemy players block you — jump over them.
