HOW TO PLAY

Chop enemies only when they cross onto YOUR side. Chopped players are out until a teammate brings the enemy flag home. Wipe out their team to win.

KEYBOARD: WASD / ARROWS move · SPACE or CLICK chop · M map · P pause
XBOX: stick move · A chop · Y map · START pause
TOUCH: drag left side to move · CHOP button · tap minimap for map

TYPE

A type · B delete · Y abc · START done

Friends need online mode. Add your Supabase URL and key to the game and play it from your GitHub Pages site.
